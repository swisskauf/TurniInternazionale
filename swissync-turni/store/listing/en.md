**Description:**
Swissync Turni turns your shift plan into worked hours, earnings and a report ready to hand in.

IMPORT YOUR PLAN AS YOU GET IT
• Photo or scan, PDF, Excel/CSV or a simple message
• On-device recognition, even offline — also tilted photos and colored cells — at no cost
• The plan's legend is read for you: hours and breaks of every shift, ready to confirm
• Finds your row among your colleagues and lets you confirm every day before saving

YOUR SHIFTS, YOUR CODES
• Define your plan's codes (e.g. P1 = 08:00–12:00 + 12:36–17:18) with breaks and night shifts
• Paste the legend and it is read automatically
• Or let the xAI assistant derive it from a photo — you confirm

ACCURATE HOURS
• Worked, night, Sunday, Saturday and public-holiday hours (Swiss cantons, Italy, Germany, France, Austria)
• Overtime and balance against your contract
• Clock in/out, actual times different from the plan, notes

PDF FOR YOUR EMPLOYER
• Detailed report with summary, days, weekly totals, legend and signatures
• Hours and times only, no amounts. In English, Italian, German or French
• Also export to CSV and your calendar

WHAT YOU EARN
• Guided setup with typical Swiss and Italian values: hourly or monthly pay, supplements, vacation, 13th salary, social contributions, withholding tax
• Or upload a payslip: the xAI assistant configures everything
• Compare the estimate with your real payslip

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES (OPTIONAL)
• Free account with email, Apple or Google: shifts synced across phone, tablet and computer
• 6 free xAI analyses a month for the hardest plans

PRIVACY FIRST
Without an account your data stays on your device. No ads, no tracking. Documents go to the xAI assistant only when you ask, and the account can be deleted from the app in seconds.

Swissync Turni is part of the Swissync platform.
